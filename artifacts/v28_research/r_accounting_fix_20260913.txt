MITEMSHUB V75 MACRO ENGINE v28 — R-ACCOUNTING / CLOSE-BOOKING FIX
Evidence date: 2026-09-13
EA: mql5/MITEMSHUB_AI/MitemshubAI_v28.mq5  (v28.00, 0 errors / 0 warnings, 5 terminal trees)
Scope: accounting only. No strategy, geometry, signal, or execution change.

===================================================================
1. WHAT WAS ASKED
===================================================================
"Log g_risk_money on every close and fix the R-accounting so cumulative R
agrees in sign with net P&L, then re-verify on one window."

Setting: the 32-record V28 protocol run showed 10 of 32 records whose cumulative
R and net P&L had OPPOSITE signs. Since OnTester() returns cumulative R, the
optimization score was not trustworthy in the high-frequency modes.

===================================================================
2. DEFECT A — R denominator (per trade). FIXED.
===================================================================
Mechanism: CaptureManagedPosition() derived the denominator from
|entry - SL| whenever the position was captured before its stops were visible.
With SL = 0 that distance is the whole price, so "risk" landed in the tens of
thousands, R collapsed toward 0 for that trade while its money still counted
against the account. Mixed with ~$100 trades, sum(R) and sum(PnL) disagreed in
sign.

Fix: single owner for the denominator. CommitTradeRisk(risk, position_id,
source) is now the only writer of g_risk_money, bound to the position id, with a
plausibility bound (RISK_PLAUSIBILITY_BOUND) and an explicit fallback source.
FinalizeClosedTrade() divides by the risk that was actually committed, and every
CLOSE line now prints the denominator and its provenance:

    CLOSE <reason> ticket=... pnl=... R=... risk=... risk_src=... cum_pnl=... cum_R=...

Measured on the first re-run (203 booked closes, is90 window):
    risk_src    : ENTRY 203 / 203   (no MISMATCH, no fallback)
    risk        : min $89.79  median $98.12  max $104.04   (was $4.39 - $79.46)
    sign(pnl) != sign(R) : 0 / 203                          (was 10 / 32 records)

===================================================================
3. DEFECT B — 77 of 262 closes never reached the counters. FIXED.
===================================================================
This is the defect that actually flipped the sign of the whole-run totals.

Mechanism (proven from the tester journal, not inferred):
Entries open on the M30 bar open and the hold window is 180 minutes = exactly
six M30 bars, so a timeout expiry ALWAYS lands on a bar open. On that tick:

    ManageTimeGuardian()  -> trade.PositionClose(...)      # tracked trade now gone
    ProcessM30Gate()      -> OpenMacroTrade() -> CaptureManagedPosition()

CaptureManagedPosition() then overwrote the tracked trade's ticket/id with the
new position. The closed trade was never booked: no CLOSE line, and no
"POSITION GONE" line either (state was replaced, not cleared).

Journal reconciliation, REVERSE_DIRECTION, M30 2026.06.12-2026.09.10, 100% real
ticks (40,320 bars / 3,885,441 ticks), BEFORE the fix:

    262  strict OPEN lines
    185  strict CLOSE lines            -> 77 closes lost
    242  distinct tickets liquidated
     77  liquidated but never booked   <- exactly the 77 lost closes
     77  OPENs sharing a journal second with a liquidation request
      0  POSITION GONE (so the loss was silent)

Report : 262 trades, net -144.81, PF 0.97
EA     : test_pnl +2826.52, test_R +29.4700   <- opposite sign to the report
Report "OnTester result": +29.47004686190031  <- the corrupted score

Fix (one owner for adoption): CaptureManagedPosition() now detects a superseded
trade (a live position with a different identifier while a trade is tracked),
books it through RecoverClosedPosition() BEFORE adopting the new position, and
if history has not exposed the deal yet it clears the stale identity instead of
carrying a stale denominator onto the new position. PollManagedPosition()'s
changed-identifier branch routes through the same capture path.

===================================================================
5. RESIDUAL / HONEST NOTES
===================================================================
* The fix repairs the accounting, not the strategy. REVERSE_DIRECTION still
  nets -144.81 (PF 0.97) on this window; nothing about the edge changed.
* -144.81 / -1.1920 implies ~$121 of risk per trade, above the ~$100 (1% of
  10k) implied by the per-close audit. The intended-risk denominator is
  equity x 1% at entry, so it moves with equity across a 262-trade run; the
  per-close audit on the 203-row sample sat at $89.79-$104.04. Not yet
  reconciled to the dollar, and not needed for the sign contract.
* g_timeout_requested is still set before the close is confirmed, so a failed
  liquidation can still mislabel a later SL exit as TIMEOUT in the reason
  column. Cosmetic; does not affect P&L or R.
* 16 of the 32 protocol records were produced by builds before this fix, so
  their cumulative R values are not comparable to post-fix records. Only
  P&L / PF / exit counts from those records remain valid. The registry should
  be re-run before any promotion decision reads cumulative R.

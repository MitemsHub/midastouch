2026-09-13

WHAT CHANGED
------------
The scorecard and promotion gate now state dollars and R as a matched pair for
every record:

  * `cumulative_r`    = sum of per-trade ratios. This is what OnTester() scores
                        and what MT5 reports as "OnTester result".
  * `money_implied_r` = net P&L / mean per-trade risk. This is the figure that
                        multiplies back to dollars.
  * `mean_risk`       = the record's own mean denominator, with `risk_source`.
  * `expectancy_r_money` = money-implied R per trade.

A verdict reason now quotes the money and the R that correspond to each other,
e.g. "out-of-sample money-implied R is not positive (-0.2021 = -20.17 USD / mean
risk 99.79)". A record with no denominator is refused outright, naming the fix
(`backfill`) instead of quietly substituting the ratio-sum figure.

WHY THE 32 RECORDS NEEDED A BACKFILL
------------------------------------
The 32 matrix records were produced before the EA emitted `R_RECONCILE`. Their
P&L, PF and ratio-sum R are trustworthy (verified earlier), but they carried no
denominator, so the gate could not convert their R to dollars.

`cmd_backfill` now resolves the denominator from each pass's own journal
segment, never another pass's. Preference order:

  1. the pass's `R_RECONCILE` line (`mean_risk`), when the build emitted one;
  2. else reconstructed from the pass's OPEN lines.

The reconstruction follows the EA's own formula: risk =
(stop_distance / tick_size) * CalibratedTickValue() * volume, and on V75 the
calibrated value is the geometric identity tick_size*contract_size (0.01 * 1.0),
so it reduces to |entry - SL| * volume.

VALIDATION OF THE RECONSTRUCTION
--------------------------------

ONE HARNESS DETAIL FIXED WHILE DOING THIS
-----------------------------------------
Storing `mean_risk` rounded to 2dp and then computing the implied R from the
UNROUNDED value left one record (V28-0016) violating
`test_pnl == money_implied_r * mean_risk` by 0.0012. The implied R is now derived
from the stored denominator, so a record's own fields satisfy the identity
exactly.

re-run already verified. What is new is that every refusal now also states a
dollar figure and an R figure that are consistent with each other, so a reader
cannot mistake the ratio-sum R for something that multiplies back to money:

  * REVERSE_DIRECTION is (the window with the largest gap): ratio -1.192 vs

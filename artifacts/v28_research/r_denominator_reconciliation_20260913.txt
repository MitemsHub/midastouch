3. ROOT CAUSE OF THE GAP — ARITHMETIC, NOT A DEFECT
===================================================================
Cumulative R is a SUM OF RATIOS. The identity

    sum(pnl_i / risk_i)  ==  sum(pnl) / mean_risk

holds only when every risk_i is identical. Measured on this run:

    mean risk                                96.859
    money-implied R  = sum(pnl)/mean_risk    -1.4951
    ratio-sum R      = sum(pnl_i/risk_i)     -1.1922
    difference (covariance term)             +0.3029   (20.3% of money-implied)
    correlation(1/risk, pnl)                 +0.053    (dispersion, not pairing)

The covariance term is exactly the difference, and the two sources of dispersion
are ordinary: equity drift (1% of equity is taken at each entry) and volume
rounding (NormalizeVolumeDown). A +/-8% risk spread is enough to move the
money-implied figure ~20% against the ratio sum with nothing wrong.

CONCLUSION: the per-trade denominator is correct and matches the close audit.
The "$122 implied risk" was the wrong statistic, not a wrong denominator.
Nothing in CommitTradeRisk / CaptureManagedPosition needed changing.

4. WHAT WAS ADDED — THE GAP IS NOW LOGGED AND BOUNDED
===================================================================
New function LogRAccounting(), called from OnTester() when InpResearchLogging
is on. It accumulates sum(risk), sum(|pnl|), min(risk) and max(risk) per close
(all reset in ResetTestMetrics so nothing leaks between passes) and emits:

  [v28.00] R_RECONCILE trades=262 mean_risk=96.86 risk_min=89.24 risk_max=104.04
           ratio_sum_r=-1.1920 money_implied_r=-1.4951 gap=+0.3030
           dispersion_bound=9.7843 consistent=true net_pnl=-144.81
           implied_risk_by_ratiosum=121.48

`consistent` is a CHECK, not a label: the gap is compared against the exact
bound of the ratio-vs-money difference,

    |sum pnl_i (1/risk_i - 1/mean_risk)|  <=  sum|pnl_i| * max_dev(1/risk)

so a genuinely wrong denominator (which would push the gap past the bound) is
reported as R_RECONCILE WARNING with the CLOSE-line risk=/risk_src= fields
named as the place to look. It cannot hide behind the benign dispersion.

Which figure to use, now stated in the code:
    ratio_sum_r      -> compare candidates (this IS what OnTester scores)
    money_implied_r  -> convert R back to dollars

===================================================================
5. THIRD HARNESS DEFECT FOUND WHILE VERIFYING
===================================================================
The research driver's `test_pnl` was `rep["pnl"]` — the report's DEAL-TABLE sum,
not the run's net profit. It drifts from both the EA counter and the report
header by ~$0.05/trade (up to $60 on a 1,200-trade run) and the bias is
one-directional, so out-of-sample P&L looked better than it was on 23 of 32
records (e.g. -129.24 recorded against -144.81 actual).

Fix: `choose_whole_run_pnl()` picks EA journal counter -> report Total Net
Profit -> deals sum, and records which one was used in a new `pnl_source` field.
Verified end-to-end on a fresh is90 V28_REVERSE_DIRECTION pass:

    trades 262 | test_pnl -144.81 | pnl_source ea_journal
    cumulative_r -1.192 | sl/tp/to 20/0/242 | research_line_present True

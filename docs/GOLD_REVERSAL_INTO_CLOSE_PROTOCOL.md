# Pre-registration: reversal into the venue's close, on the slice the momentum study did not consume

**Date declared:** 2026-09-21 · **Declared before:** `scripts/gold_reversal_into_close.py` was run
· **Artifact:** `artifacts/gold_reversal_into_close.json` · **Verdict:**
`docs/GOLD_REVERSAL_INTO_CLOSE_VERDICT_20260921.md`

One hypothesis, one primary sample, plus one pre-declared descriptive auxiliary. The rule below
is written before the measurement because the hypothesis was generated by data that is already
consumed, and a second look at the same days is not a test.

## Where the hypothesis comes from, and why it cannot be tested where it came from

`docs/GOLD_INTRADAY_MOMENTUM_VERDICT_20260921.md` measured continuation into the venue's close
and found the net effect **negative in the +120 era** (net **−0.0210R/day**, t −2.00, n 118),
positive but insignificant in the +60 era (+0.0306R/day, t +1.19, n 29), and flat pooled. The
tempting reading is "so trade the other way". Two things must be said about that before the
first number is computed here:

1. **Those 147 days are consumed.** The momentum study already looked at this window's outcomes.
   Re-testing the reverse direction on the same outcomes is a second look wearing a new sign,
   and this program refuses it. The consumed window is cited only as the *reason the hypothesis
   exists*.
2. **Cost is paid in both directions.** Reversing the trade flips the gross term and leaves the
   toll untouched: on the consumed +120 era, `gross −0.0128R` becomes `+0.0128R` while `cost
   0.0082R` stays, so the reversal-direction advantage there is **+0.0046R/day** — five times
   smaller than the −0.0210R *net* figure that made the hint look interesting. The +60 era
   reverses to −0.0306R/day net. That arithmetic is stated here, before the run, because it is
   the difference between "a −2 t-stat" and "a fifth of a tenth of an R".

## Declared hypothesis (H2, directional)

> The return from the venue's day anchor to the start of the final 30 minutes of that venue-day
> predicts the final-30-minute return **with the opposite sign** (`b < 0` in
> `r_LH' = a + b·r_ROD'`); equivalently, the tradable claim is that entering **against** the
> day's move into the venue's close earns a positive net return after the recorded spread.

Sign declared now. A positive, significant slope **rejects** H2 and confirms the momentum
direction instead; it is not a licence to re-report the momentum study's result.

## Declared primary sample — the unconsumed slice, defined structurally

The consumed set is the day list inside `artifacts/gold_intraday_momentum.json`. The primary
sample is every **stamped day of the venue's M15 gold series that is NOT in that list** and that
satisfies all of:

| condition | definition |
|---|---|
| day anchor present at the standard open | the day's **00:00** stamp bar exists (the venue's own day open, after the 23:00 break) |
| the venue's close moved | the day's **final** stamp is earlier than 22:30, i.e. the venue closed before its standard final pair — those are the days the momentum study skipped for completeness |
| enough of the day to have a "day so far" | at least 40 stamped bars |
| final 30 minutes exist | the last two stamped bars close the final 30 minutes (two 15-minute bars) |

MEASURED STRUCTURE, BEFORE ANY RETURN WAS COMPUTED (enumerating bars only, no prices): of 179
stamped days, 147 were consumed and **32 are unconsumed**; among those, 22 carry a 00:00 anchor
and 10 do not (8 start at 01:00, one at 01:15, one at 13:15 — the series' own first day), and the
unconsumed days' final pairs are spread over nine different stamp pairs (21:30→21:45 ×17,
22:30→22:45 ×6, 21:45→22:00 ×2, 17:45→18:00 ×2, and five singletons). The unconsumed days are
concentrated in January (12) and March (15), with five scattered later — i.e. mostly US
early-close days in the +60 era.

This is stated as a *weakness* rather than a feature: the primary sample is small, single-era
and structurally unlike the consumed one. It is nevertheless the only gold slice on this venue
that the momentum study did not see.

## Declared auxiliary (descriptive only, declared as such in advance)

All 32 unconsumed days, anchors as available (the day's first stamped bar). Its p-value is
**descriptive**, not confirmatory: the anchor moves between 00:00, 01:00, 01:15 and 13:15 across
those days, so they are not one population, and the heterogeneity was chosen by the data's own
completeness rather than by a rule about the market.

## Declared construction (identical to the momentum protocol except where stated)

| element | definition |
|---|---|
| anchor `A` | the day's 00:00 bar (primary) / first stamped bar (auxiliary) |
| `L-1`, `L` | the day's final two stamped bars |
| `r_ROD'` | `open(L-1) / open(A) − 1` |
| `r_LH'` | `close(L) / open(L-1) − 1` |
| direction | **against** `sign(r_ROD')` — the declared reversal |
| entry / exit | open of `L-1` / close of `L` |
| skip | `r_ROD' == 0`; any required bar missing |
| stop | `2.0 × ATR(H1, 14)` at the entry instant (the arm's geometry), R = net ÷ stop |
| cost | half the **recorded** spread of each side, floored at `midas_sweep.SPREAD_FLOOR = $0.10` |
| era offset | per month from `configs/mt5/server_offsets.json`; a month containing a DST step is refused, never defaulted |
| decision rule | **PASS** iff pooled mean net R > 0 **and** t ≥ 1.96 **and** every era mean ≥ 0; **FAIL** when the effect is at or above the minimum detectable effect at 80% power; otherwise **UNDECIDED AT THIS N** |

## Declared required sample — computed before the run, from published numbers only

Using the momentum artifact's published per-era dispersion (sd ≈ 0.114R/day in the +120 era,
0.120R/day pooled) and the declared MDE formula `(1.96 + 0.8416)·sd/√n`:

| effect to detect | days required | ≈ years at 1 observation/day |
|---|---|---|
| **0.0046R/day** (the cost-inclusive reversal hint) | **≈ 4,800** | ≈ 19 |
| 0.0109R/day (the momentum study's observed net) | ≈ 950 | ≈ 3.9 |
| 0.068R/day (what 22 days can see) | 22 | 0.1 |

**Declared consequence:** the available unconsumed gold slice (~22 days) can distinguish an
effect of about **0.068R/day** and nothing smaller. The hypothesis's own hint, once costs are
paid in both directions, is 0.0046R/day — fifteen times below what this sample can see.
Therefore the only possible honest verdicts here are **UNDECIDED AT THIS N**, or a PASS/FAIL if
the effect is unexpectedly large. No smaller reading may be reported as "no edge", and the
consumed window may not be re-used to supply the missing power.

## Declared forward primary

The one direction that is genuinely un-consumed is **forward**: the venue's close on
2026-09-21 and every trading day after it. That sample does not exist yet (one observation
tonight at most), so it cannot be tested here and is declared for accumulation:
`python scripts/gold_reversal_into_close.py --forward` will include post-2026-09-21 days as the
venue serves them. At the declared 0.0046R/day effect it would need ~4,800 observations; at a
1.0R/trade-scale effect (0.02R/day) it would need ~250. Both numbers are stated now so a
forward PASS cannot be claimed early.

## Limits, declared before the numbers

1. **Early-close days are not the same regime.** Their last 30 minutes sit an hour or more
   earlier in the day, often around a US holiday when liquidity is thin. A reversal that exists
   at 20:15 UTC need not exist at 21:15 UTC, and vice versa.
2. **Single era, single instrument, one 8-month history.** No pass here licenses arming
   anything, and a fail says nothing about gold elsewhere.
3. **The sign came from this window's own history.** H2 is data-inspired; that is exactly why
   it is tested on the days the inspiration did not cover, and why the auxiliary is labelled
   descriptive in advance.
